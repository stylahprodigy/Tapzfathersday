ENTRY III • THE TIMELINE

a reel of the years

2009

Two of Us

A photo taken too close, too bright, too fast — and therefore exactly right. This was how he loved: near enough to blur the edges, bright enough to make us squint. We kept it because it kept us.

2006

Held, in the Light

Every good photograph of him is overexposed, as if the camera refused to hold a man that bright. She leaned in, the light came through the window, and the whole afternoon became a kind of grace.

2007

The Glow Between Them

Two people who had learned, over years, to live in each other's light. The flare in the lens was only the sun doing what their faces had already done — refusing to let us tell where one ended and the other began.

2014

What He Grew

Not crops, not wealth — people. A girl who learned to run in his shadow, a child who learned to sit on his shoulders. The tattoo told of where he'd been; the children told of where he stayed.
